## Application

`src/data/yachts/release.json` pins the repository and full revision. Twenty matching, checksum-verified manifests are bundled under `public/yachts/`; large binaries remain external. The fleet contains 49 entries: the previous 29 vessels plus the 20 new yachts. Octopus remains one of the 29 and retains its route and model.

The public draft keeps its draft status in the catalog, fleet cards and reconstruction notes. Manifest requests for legacy vessels remain local; yacht manifests are bundled locally so text and semantic data remain available when the Hub is unavailable. Yacht binary and thumbnail requests use the pinned public dataset revision.

The yacht interior navigation panel now measures the title and view toolbar before positioning itself. It reserves space above the canvas and no longer overlaps those controls on compact screens.

## Outstanding quality work

Independent QA reports `research/yachts/independent-qa-pass54.md` and `independent-qa-pass55.md` remain changes-required. Known issues include simplified silhouettes, planar glazing and stacked deck forms, sparse interiors, primitive equipment and fittings, material and support details, seams and some room camera framing. The dataset card explicitly discloses these limitations. Dimensions and structural/export checks alone are not evidence of a faithful finished reconstruction. Desktop/mobile performance targets on documented hardware and the full per-room visual acceptance suite are not certified by this draft release.

## Reproducing the app pin

Download the immutable public snapshot into the local validation cache. Then run:

```sh
node --import tsx scripts/yachts/pin.ts \
  --snapshot .tools/yachts/verified/962dabe349121eed93a593634e5c6aa0e7c8ec38 \
  --repository cristianexer/hullscope-yachts \
  --revision 962dabe349121eed93a593634e5c6aa0e7c8ec38 \
  --allow-draft
```

This validates all release checksums before generating the catalog pin and copying lightweight manifests. Routine builds use those bundled files and do not download the collection or Blender masters.
