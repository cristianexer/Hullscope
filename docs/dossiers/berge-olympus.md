## Geometry quality controls
Functional weather-ventilation assemblies and physical searchlights include serviceable parts with distinct purposes; their exact number and location are reconstructed. Repeated grille bars, skins and support rails are decorative and excluded from the meaningful inventory. Added equipment follows its physical supporting deck during disassembly, including cross-system children.

Family-specific hull planforms use closed raked stems and outward-facing normals. Upper and underwater paint regions are complementary sections of one station surface with identical transforms and shared seam normals, avoiding an overlapping scaled lower hull. Deckhouse dimensions and storey heights are authored separately by vessel architecture. Rail segments follow deck edges and structural beams use endpoint-derived rotations. Deck-indexed disassembly vectors separate storeys, with glazing and fittings linked to their support layers; roof equipment moves above the lifted roof. Below-deck component bounds are fitted within the illustrative hull envelope. Battery vessels use electric drive schematics; the high-speed catamaran uses waterjet outlines. These controls improve physical coherence, not survey accuracy.
