# Sunseeker batch 03

This batch adds two older, generation-qualified Sunseeker production profiles that overlap the 2006–2026 project period.

## Predator 57 Mk II

YachtBuyer separates the 2017–2019 Mk II from the 2015–2017 predecessor and publishes the Mk II’s 18.24 m length, 4.70 m beam, 1.30 m draft, 2,200 L fuel, 600 L fresh water, three cabins, six guests, three-head arrangement, 325 nm range and twin Volvo engine options. Sunseeker’s previous-model archive and a period brochure provide manufacturer identity and layout context. A delivered-vessel listing is retained as an independent dimension cross-check.

## Manhattan 63

YachtBuyer dates the Manhattan 63 to 2010–2015 and reports 21.07 m LOA, 5.13 m beam, 1.60 m full-load draft, four cabins, up to eight guests, 33-knot maximum speed, 22-knot cruise and 809 nm range. A YachtBuyer comparison, HMY brokerage guide and Sunseeker archive provide independent and manufacturer context. Tank and engine-option fields not exposed consistently by those sources remain null.

Both profiles are reference-informed reconstructions. Exact build counts, owner-specific layouts and unreported technical values are not invented.
