# Sunseeker yacht generation research — batch 01

Checked: 14 September 2026  
Scope: exactly the eight requested canonical Sunseeker generations. This batch is research only; no geometry or application code was built or changed.

## Evidence method

Official Sunseeker model pages and brochures are the canonical owners of dimensional and technical claims. Independent YachtBuyer, YachtWorld, Yachting, Motor Boat & Yachting, Power Boating and BoatTEST material is used to cross-check model years, generation boundaries, production status, review-level interiors and real-world naming. Conflicts are preserved in both the JSON facts and the notes below rather than normalized away.

The two machine-readable and narrative outputs deliberately contain links only. No source photographs or prose have been redistributed.

## Profile findings

### Superhawk 55 — current

Sunseeker's current [Superhawk 55 page](https://www.sunseeker.com/range/superhawk-55) is direct evidence for 17.13 m LOA, 4.93 m beam and 1.43 m draft. It also publishes two cabins, two en-suites/dayhead, twin Volvo Penta IPS 950 pods and up to 38 knots. The page's profile separates exterior, main deck and lower deck, and its interior description supports a forward VIP, aft master and open-plan lobby/galley. The observed [official Superhawk 55 brochure](https://uploads.sunseeker.com/uploads/e4ccda224055627bdbe1e1b3112bef1a/en/Sunseeker_Superhawk55_400x225_Web.pdf) adds side-elevation, exterior-plan, main-deck and lower-deck general-arrangement evidence; its older 17.15 m / 4.95 m technical block remains a documented conflict rather than being averaged away.

The [YachtBuyer review](https://www.yachtbuyer.com/en-gb/reviews/sunseeker-superhawk-55-2022) identifies the boat as a 2023 model year and in production, and independently describes the two double cabins with en-suites. The independent [YachtWorld launch feature](https://www.yachtworld.com/research/sunseeker-superhawk-55-new-legend-on-the-water/) reports materially different rounded dimensions, 17.3 m and 4.6 m; those remain a reported conflict and are not used to replace the official figures.

Reference visual cues are the low open-top profile, large aft cockpit wet bar, movable cockpit furniture, hydraulic bathing platform/tender garage and retractable/open screen treatment. The model is a current production profile, but no factory production-start press release was found; 2023 is the independent model-year anchor.

### Hawk 38 — historical

The official [previous-model page](https://www.sunseeker.com/es/yachts-and-boats/previous-models/hawk-38) and [Hawk 38 brochure](https://uploads.sunseeker.com/uploads/436ac2736ce5c504703c6e53d9f76c15/en/Sunseeker_Hawk38_400x225_wEB.pdf) give 11.85 m LOA and 3.01 m beam, twin Mercury outboard propulsion and a 62-knot published target. The official technical block publishes one head but lists cabin count as N/A; that missing cabin evidence is retained rather than inferred. Draft is also not published in the accessible official material.

Independent [Yachting coverage](https://www.yachtingmagazine.com/speedy-sunseeker/) and [Motor Boat & Yachting first-look coverage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZnFgI0dY9hs) place the model in the 2018 launch era and support its racing-derived, open dayboat identity. The official site now exposes it as a previous model, but no reliable end year was found. It is therefore historical with `productionEnd: null`.

The visual block should remain an open center-console/dayboat silhouette with twin outboards and one head. There is not enough direct interior evidence to model enclosed cabins.

### Manhattan 55 — current Gen 2

The current [Manhattan 55 technical page](https://www.sunseeker.com/en/yachts-and-boats/yacht/manhattan-55) supplies direct LOA/beam/draft of 17.21/4.87/1.37 m, three cabins, two en-suites/dayhead and up to 32 knots with shaft or pod options. It describes the galley-aft arrangement, open-plan social deck and Beach Club transom.

The independent [YachtBuyer review](https://www.yachtbuyer.com/en-us/reviews/sunseeker-manhattan-55-2021-2) identifies the requested profile as Gen 2, model year 2021 and in production. A second [YachtBuyer/Aquaholic review](https://www.yachtbuyer.com/en-us/reviews/sunseeker-manhattan-55-2021-aquaholic) provides interior and machinery walkthrough evidence. It reports a 1.26 m full-load draft versus the official page's 1.37 m, and approximately 250 nm versus the official 230 nm at 21 knots; these are preserved as load/configuration differences.

This profile must not be merged with the older Manhattan 55 page in Sunseeker's previous-model/localized archive, which carries a different dimensional block. The `gen2-2021` ID makes that boundary explicit.

### Manhattan 68 — current 2025 generation

The current [Manhattan 68 page](https://www.sunseeker.com/range/manhattan-68) directly publishes 21.21 m LOA, 5.26 m beam and 1.60 m draft, plus 4 cabins and 3 en-suites/dayhead. Its official layout text supports both a four-cabin reference and a three-cabin configuration with a private office. The current technical block gives twin MAN V8 1000/V8 1200 shaft options, up to 32 knots and 550 nm at 10 knots.

The [YachtBuyer 2025 premiere report](https://www.yachtbuyer.com/en-gb/news/sunseeker-manhattan-68-to-premiere-at-genoa-international-boat-show-2025) says the boat was delivered in 2025 and had its show premiere at Genoa. The official page also labels the next-generation model `(2025)`. That is sufficient to treat the requested current Manhattan 68 as a distinct `gen2-2025` profile.

There is an older official [Manhattan 68 brochure](https://uploads.sunseeker.com/uploads/70b2a9ae973cc90459c0f7de57a23062/en/Sunseeker_Manhattan68_400x225_Web.pdf) with a different 37,400 kg displacement and shafts-or-pods wording. It is retained as a historical publication conflict, not silently merged into the current 2025 specification.

### Predator 65 — current

The current [Predator 65 page](https://www.sunseeker.com/range/predator-65) directly gives 20.50 m LOA, 5.10 m beam, 1.60 m draft, 3 cabins with a 4-cabin option, 4 en-suites/dayhead, twin Volvo Penta IPS 1200/1350 pods, up to 35 knots and 750 nm at 10 knots.

The independent [YachtBuyer review](https://www.yachtbuyer.com/en/reviews/sunseeker-predator-65-2021) identifies a 2021 model year, in-production status and the key generation relationship: the Predator 65 arrived after the 65 Sport Yacht, omitting the sportsbridge while adding a large retractable sunroof. [Yachting's review](https://www.yachtingmagazine.com/yachts/sunseeker-predator-65-reviewed/) independently supports the sunroof and Volvo IPS configuration.

The reference interior is therefore a two-level sportscruiser with main-deck cockpit/helm/saloon, retractable roof and a lower deck containing a full-beam owner suite plus two guest cabins. The 65 Sport Yacht remains a separate product; shared platform dimensions are not treated as evidence that the names are aliases.

### Predator 74 — Gen 2, introduced 2018

The 2018 boundary is unusually well supported. [Sunseeker Brokerage's Boot Düsseldorf report](https://www.sunseekerbrokerage.com/2018/01/28/boat-show-another-successful-year-at-boot-dusseldorf/) identifies the Predator 74 as one of the two new model launches at the January 2018 show. The official [Predator 74 brochure](https://www.sunseeker.com/uploads/150ea3de88828eaafd9c67fdba59df29/en/Sunseeker_Predator74_400x225_Web.pdf) gives direct 22.82 m LOA and 5.38 m beam evidence. The official previous-model technical block gives 1.83 m draft, three cabins with an optional fourth, three heads, shaft propulsion and up to 40 knots.

The independent [YachtBuyer model page](https://www.yachtbuyer.com/en-us/sunseeker/new/predator-74) explicitly labels this boat `Gen 2, 2018-2023`, and says it is discontinued. That end year is retained as reported, not elevated to factory-certified. Independent [Power Boating coverage](https://www.powerboating.com/sunseeker-predator-74/) describes the 2018 reintroduction, oversized pillarless windows, full-beam master and upper-30/40-knot performance.

The performance conflict is important: official material says 40 knots, YachtBuyer standard data says 34 knots, and an independent 2018 brochure/listing says 35 knots. These likely reflect engine options, load and test conditions; the JSON keeps all evidence visible. The earlier 2009–2012 Predator 74 is a different generation and is explicitly excluded from this profile.

### 90 Ocean — first Ocean generation

The current [90 Ocean page](https://www.sunseeker.com/range/90-ocean) gives direct 27.10 m LOA, 7.16 m beam and 1.94 m draft, with 4 cabins, 4 en-suites/dayhead, up to 27 knots and 1,100 nm at 12 knots. The official [Ocean introduction announcement](https://www.sunseeker.de/news/news/sunseeker-introduces-a-new-range-to-its-growing-family-of-luxury-performance-motor-yachts-1) says the first Ocean model, the 90 Ocean, was introduced in 2020. Independent [YachtBuyer review](https://www.yachtbuyer.com/en/reviews/sunseeker-90-ocean-2021) uses model year 2021 and confirms the four-cabin layout, full-beam owner suite, open/enclosed upper-deck choice and the X-TEND transom concept.

Independent [Motor Boat & Yachting coverage](https://www.mby.com/news/sunseeker-90-yacht-superyacht-series-107229) and the [YachtBuyer review](https://www.yachtbuyer.com/en/reviews/sunseeker-90-ocean-2021) provide strong interior and deck evidence: broad high-volume beam, two-tier aft terrace, hydraulic bathing/tender platform, X-TEND transom, open flybridge and optional enclosed upper deck. The independent sources also report 1.86 m draft and 1,570 L fresh water versus official 1.94 m and 1,500 L; both are retained as configuration/source conflicts.

The requested 90 Ocean and separately exposed 90 Ocean Enclosed are treated as open/enclosed siblings of one first-generation Ocean profile, not as two of the eight generation profiles.

### 131 Yacht — historical tri-deck

The official [131 Yacht brochure](https://uploads.sunseeker.com/uploads/18b22a2751695f2b335ea589b20abceb/en/Sunseeker_131Yacht_400x225_Web.pdf) is direct evidence for 40.05 m LOA, 8.09 m beam, 2.85 m draft, MTU shaft propulsion, up to 25 knots and up to 1,600 nm at 12 knots. It describes a tri-deck yacht, provides general-arrangement pages and states up to 12 guests and 9 crew, but does not force one cabin count because the design is semi-custom.

The independent [YachtBuyer model page](https://www.yachtbuyer.com/en/sunseeker/new/131-yacht) describes the 131 as a discontinued 2016–2024 model, with five en-suite cabins, a main-deck owner suite, sky lounge/private deck and crew quarters. [YachtBuyer's 2016 review](https://www.yachtbuyer.com/en-us/reviews/sunseeker-131-yacht-2016-aquaholic) reports a six-cabin review boat and gives clear deck-by-deck interior evidence including the owner suite, sky lounge, pilothouse, crew mess, tender garage and sundeck. [YachtWorld's launch-era report](https://www.yachtworld.com/research/sunseeker-131-yacht/) adds order-book context before the first 2016 hulls touched water.

The cabin conflict is retained rather than flattened: five guest cabins is the model-page reference, while six cabins is reported for a review boat. Likewise, official 40.05 m LOA and independent 40.06 m are kept as a rounding discrepancy.

## Evidence quality summary

- **Direct LOA and beam:** verified from an official Sunseeker page or brochure for all eight profiles.
- **Draft:** official and usable for seven profiles; Hawk 38 remains unknown because the accessible official technical block does not publish it.
- **Interior/layout:** clear official and/or independent evidence for all eight, but Hawk 38 has no published cabin count and 131 Yacht is explicitly semi-custom.
- **Generation/status:** strongest for Manhattan 55 Gen 2 (2021), Manhattan 68 (2025), Predator 74 Gen 2 (2018–2023 reported), 90 Ocean introduction (2020/2021 model-year distinction), and 131 Yacht (2016–2024 reported). Superhawk 55 and Predator 65 start years are model-year anchors rather than factory production ledger dates; Hawk 38's 2018 start is launch-era evidence.
- **Conflicts retained:** Superhawk independent rounded dimensions; Manhattan 55 draft/range; Manhattan 68 older official technical block; Predator 74 speed; 90 Ocean draft/fresh water; 131 Yacht LOA/cabin count.
- **Scope check:** JSON contains exactly eight model profiles and no geometry assets were created.
