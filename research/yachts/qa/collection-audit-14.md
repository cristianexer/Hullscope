# Collection audit 14

## Verdict

The collection remains **changes-required / release hold**. Publication is not allowed: `0/20` have independent approval and all 20 draft quality records remain `changes-required` with `independent-qa-pending`.

The latest exterior pass is a real partial improvement. Eighteen generic master/GLB/render sets now show visible family/model cues, including the lower V40 treatment, taller F/Y/X arrangements, sportsbridge S variants and Predator brow/intake details. The raised sheer and Princess/Sunseeker palette split are also visible. They do not yet establish reference-faithful hull, glazing, roofline, deck or material fidelity; the fleet still reads as simplified procedural shells. The room evidence remains blockout-level.

## Visual findings

The refreshed exterior sheet no longer supports the strongest version of the previous “all models are identical” finding. The new family signatures are visible and useful. However, the changes are mostly added slabs, brows, rails, sunpads, blades and window strips around a still-simplified hull/superstructure vocabulary. Glazing remains low-detail and dark, deck/roof transitions remain schematic, and the new material palette is a color separation rather than convincing gelcoat, glass, teak and upholstery response. No yacht clears the distinctive-features or materials gates.

The shared two-side-wall cutaway rule remains verified for all `20/20` room sets and `111/111` current room frames. It does not make the interiors release-ready: rooms remain simplified beds, sofas, partitions and basic equipment in shallow/open shells, with residual forward/roof/bulkhead occlusion and tight diagonal framing in several views. V55 saloon remains furniture-like rather than a convincing deck-specific interior.

## Blockers

- **VIS-001 — exterior fidelity:** model-level signatures are visible in 18 refreshed sets, but reference-faithful hull curvature, sheer/chines, glazing boundaries, roofline transitions, deck geometry and distinctive fittings remain unproven. All 20 draft distinctive-features checks fail.
- **VIS-002 — room fidelity and readability:** the two-side-wall rule is applied across all 20 room sets, but all remain blockout-level. Enclosures, joinery, fixtures, machinery and physical supports are incomplete; residual occlusion and tight framing remain.
- **VIS-003 — materials:** Princess/Sunseeker palette separation and 128×128 texture payloads are present, but the renders still read largely as flat color blocks with low-detail glazing, gelcoat, teak and interior response. Draft textures and materials-in-all-views checks fail on every record.
- **APP-001 — approval:** no selected yacht has explicit independent approval; technical visual review is pending and `publicationReady` is false for all 20.
